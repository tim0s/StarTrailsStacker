Privacy Policy

Star Trail Stacker for Mac · Effective 15 October 2026

In short: Star Trail Stacker does not collect, send or share any data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no network connections. Your photos and everything the app makes from them stay on your Mac.

Who is responsible

Star Trail Stacker is developed by Timo Schneider (“I”, “me”). If you have questions about this policy, write to tmschneider748@gmail.com.

What the app does with your photos

Star Trail Stacker turns a series of night-sky photos into a star-trail image and a timelapse. To do that it:

  • reads the image files (RAW, TIFF or JPEG) in the folder you choose, and only that folder;
  • reads information stored in those files, such as capture time, exposure time, aperture, ISO and focal length, to show them in the app and to work out the timing between frames;
  • processes the images entirely on your Mac.

Your photos and their information are never uploaded or sent anywhere.

Files the app stores on your Mac

  • Intermediate files: developed previews, cleaned full-resolution frames and stack data. They are kept in the app’s caches folder or in a folder you choose in Settings, and can be deleted at any time in Settings › Intermediate Files › Delete Intermediate Files.
  • Results: the final image and timelapse are saved only where you choose to save them.
  • Project files (.startrails), saved only where you choose. They contain your settings, your corrections of the landscape mask, the location of your frames folder, and for each frame its file name, size and a checksum, so the app can tell whether frames changed since you saved. They do not contain your photos.
  • Preferences: the folder for intermediate files and your choice of grid or table view, stored in the app’s preferences on your Mac.

All of these stay on your Mac (or on the disk you choose) and are under your control. Deleting the app and its folders removes them.

Data collected

None. Star Trail Stacker contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party services, and it does not connect to the internet. I receive no information about you or how you use the app.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen in macOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide me with anonymous crash reports and diagnostics for Star Trail Stacker. This is controlled by Apple and by your settings in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements; see Apple’s privacy policy. These reports do not include your photos.

Purchases

If you get Star Trail Stacker from the Mac App Store, the purchase is handled by Apple. I do not receive your name, email address or payment details.

Children

Star Trail Stacker collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because I do not collect or hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete. Everything the app stores is on your own Mac, where you can delete it yourself. You are welcome to contact me with any privacy question.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Star Trail Stacker changes how it handles data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
